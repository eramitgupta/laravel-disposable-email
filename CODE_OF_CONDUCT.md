# Code of Conduct

We want **Laravel Disposable Email Address Validator** to be a welcoming place for developers to contribute, learn, and share ideas.

Whether you're reporting a bug, suggesting a feature, reviewing code, or submitting a pull request, please treat others with respect.

## Our Standards

We encourage everyone to:

- Be respectful, friendly, and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives, experiences, and skill levels.
- Give helpful feedback and be open to receiving it.
- Respect disagreements and discuss them professionally.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, and solutions rather than individuals.
- Assume good intentions and communicate clearly.

Everyone should feel comfortable asking questions and participating in the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or abusive language.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Repeatedly making disrespectful or inappropriate comments.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or notice behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers privately through an appropriate available contact channel.

When reporting an incident, include relevant details so the situation can be reviewed fairly.

Reports will be handled with care, and information will be shared only as necessary to investigate and address the situation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a respectful and productive environment.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may:

- Ask contributors to change or stop inappropriate behavior.
- Edit or remove offensive comments.
- Close or lock discussions that become disruptive.
- Reject contributions that violate these guidelines.
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions will consider the nature and severity of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the project's GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project communication channels.

## Our Goal

Open source works best when people can share knowledge, ask questions, and contribute without unnecessary negativity.

You don't have to agree with everyone, but you are expected to treat others respectfully.

**Thank you for helping keep this project a positive place for the Laravel community.**
